Pulse Pocket

Real-time Solana narrative intelligence — in your pocket, with wallet-native action.

Core thesis
Attention precedes liquidity. Narratives move before price does.

Problem

By the time you see the move, it already moved.

Hype cycles outrun price, and most "mobile" crypto tools are just websites in a shell — no wallet, no awareness of your phone, no moment-of-momentum action.

Noise > signal
Thousands of tokens pumping daily. Feeds are firehoses; nobody is telling you why something moved.
Hype precedes price
Attention lands on a token before liquidity does. By the time it's on every dashboard, the entry is gone.
Web apps in a shell
Wrappers can't alert you with the app killed, can't use your mobile wallet natively, and can't act in one tap.
No risk screen
Pumps get hyped. Most "AI alpha" never checks the contract for the rug pulled just after the narrative.
Solution

A narrative radar that lives on your phone.

One loop — listen, understand, act, stay alerted — wired end-to-end with real data.

Listen
Polls live Solana price/volume from DexScreener every 90s. Detects price spikes (≥8% 1h / ≥25% 24h) and volume spikes (≥2x a rolling on-device baseline).
Understand
Rug-screens the mover on-chain via Birdeye, reads an attention-proxy, and writes a short plain-language narrative from those real facts with an LLM.
Act
Connect your Solana mobile wallet and swap through Jupiter in one tap — on the narrative card, no leaving the feed.
Stay alerted
A native background task fires a real spike alert with the app fully killed. Tapping it deep-links straight back into that token.

Mobile-first, Solana-native

Built for the phone, not ported to it.

  • Mobile Wallet Adapter — native wallet connect on a development build (MWA can't run in Expo Go; this is a real native app).
  • One-tap Jupiter swap executed on-device from the narrative card itself.
  • Alert with the app killed — TaskManager + BackgroundFetch reusing the spike detector; no server, no push provider.
  • Tap the alert → back into that token — deep-linked straight to the pair's risk screen and swap.
  • OTA updates — new versions ship over the air to installed APKs (expo-updates + EAS Update).
  • Android-first, Seeker-ready — a working internal-release APK, installable today.
